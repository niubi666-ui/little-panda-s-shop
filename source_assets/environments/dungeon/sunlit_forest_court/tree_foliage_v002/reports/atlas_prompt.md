# 枝叶图集生成记录

工具：内置 imagegen；透明背景。参考为用户本轮提供的圣树整图。最终像素尺寸以 build_report.json 为准，不宣称 2K。

## Prompt

Create a production foliage texture atlas with actual transparent background, square image, four equal quadrants in a precise 2x2 grid without visible grid lines. Each quadrant contains ONE distinct compact irregular rounded oak leafy twig spray, completely inside its quadrant with a 5 percent empty margin. Use the attached ancient oak ONLY as foliage style reference, not the whole tree. Each spray contains 35-50 SMALL SHORT BROAD ROUNDED LOBED oak leaves, foliage characteristic of this reference: mature olive green, muted yellow-green tips, dark green undersides, subtle brown twigs, natural mottling, realistic fine veins. Each individual leaf length about 1.4 to 1.7 times width, rounded lobes, not lance-shaped, not serrated spikes, not feathers, not fern fronds. Branchlets subdivide irregularly into three or four directions, creating squat bushy rounded sprays with interior transparent gaps and no regular bilateral fishbone arrangement. Each spray viewed straight-on orthographic, approximately flat for mesh card mapping, tiny natural overlaps and curved individual leaves give convincing surface detail. Top of each spray is up, short twig attachment at bottom center. All four equally sized and centered at exactly quadrant centers (25%,25%), (75%,25%), (25%,75%), (75%,75%). Soft neutral diffuse illumination, NO cast shadows on background, no ambient background, no ground, no text, no labels, no borders. High fidelity realistic botanical game texture, natural subtle shading and leaf texture, avoid flat cartoon colors, no whole tree, no pots, no long branches. Accurate alpha cutout.
